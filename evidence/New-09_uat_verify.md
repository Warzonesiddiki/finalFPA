## 6. Root Cause and Recommendations

1. **Root Cause:**
   The author of `packaging/uat_dry_run_report.md` mistook the isolated unit-fixture in `tests/uat/test_uat_dry_run.py` for a full integration run against the physical serving files in `sample-data/`. The report narrated the fixture's 3-row numbers (12.5M, 5.2M, 1.6M) as if they were derived from `d365_gl_actuals.csv` (which actually contains ~190M–201M per line).
2. **Corrective Action Required (for future wave):**
